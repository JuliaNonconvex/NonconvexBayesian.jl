Zero-order surrogates

ZeroOrderGPSurrogate models an expensive function f with one GP per output. In :exact mode it evaluates f and records the observation; in :interval mode it returns the acquisition interval (see Acquisitions). The surrogate constructor evaluates f at the initial point and switches to interval mode.

s = surrogate(f, x0)                    # interval mode, LCB by default
s = surrogate(f, x0; acquisition = EI()) # expected improvement
lo = get_lo(s(x))                       # lower bound of the interval

The surrogate callable carries a ChainRules rule: in exact mode its gradient is the gradient of the wrapped function; in interval mode the gradient is the chain through the acquisition interval (the lower bound get_lo path). Gradients are therefore available to first-order sub-optimizers through Zygote without differentiating IntervalArithmetic's internals.

Hyperparameter fitting

The GP hyperparameters are refitted by maximum likelihood every every observations after the first skip * every observations (see Options). Non-finite observations are skipped with a warning instead of poisoning the GP; the posterior is cached across interval-mode evaluations and only rebuilt when the data or the GP change.

Surrogate models and the surrogate machinery

AbstractGPSurrogate is the supertype of the zero-order (ZeroOrderGPSurrogate) and first-order (FirstOrderGPSurrogate) Gaussian-process surrogates.

surrogate_model builds the full surrogate subproblem for a VecModel: it replaces every :expensive-flagged objective and constraint by a GP surrogate (LCB for the objective, conservative LCB for constraints), leaving cheap functions, bounds and semidefinite constraints untouched, and returns (sm, surrogates):

vm, x0, _ = NonconvexCore.tovecmodel(m, [1.0, 2.0])
sm, surrogates = surrogate_model(vm)             # zero-order surrogates
sm2, surrogates2 = surrogate_model(vm; first_order = true)   # gradient-enhanced

The returned surrogates are the callables whose X/y fields record the query history. After the sub-optimizer proposes a candidate x from the surrogate model, update_surrogates! evaluates the true model at x with the surrogates in exact mode — recording the observations (values and gradients) — and returns (objective, ineq_constraints, eq_constraints) at x:

o, i, e = update_surrogates!(m, surrogates, x)

This is exactly the loop BayesOptAlg runs per iteration; calling the two functions directly is useful when building custom BO loops on top of the package.
